In short

This public website uses no advertising, profiling, or non-essential cookies and does not load third-party analytics or web fonts. Offline PRG32 games stay on your device. Personal data is processed only when you use an optional connected feature such as the Cartridge Store, Construction Kit, multiplayer, leaderboards, uploads, or support.

01

Who is responsible

The PRG32 project determines why and how personal data is processed for the Services described below and is the data controller for that processing. The project is coordinated by Raffaele Montella in Naples, Italy.

Privacy requests can be sent to raffaele.montella@uniparthenope.it. If a particular deployment, school, cartridge author, or third-party service determines its own purposes and means, that party may be a separate controller and must provide its own notice.

02

Scope

This notice covers the PRG32 public website, PRG32 applications and games, the Cartridge Store, the Construction Kit, and related online features operated by the PRG32 project (the “Services”).

Community cartridges and linked websites may have independent processing and privacy notices. Their policies apply when they determine how your data is used.

03

Data and sources

Data you provide

  • account data, such as email address, account identifier, verification state, and authentication credentials;
  • profile, cartridge, source, artwork, title, description, and other content you choose to upload or publish;
  • player name or identifier, scores, leaderboard entries, and multiplayer session data;
  • support messages, privacy requests, and any information you include in them; and
  • optional diagnostics or performance metrics you knowingly submit.

Data collected automatically

When you connect to an online Service, servers may receive the IP address, date and time, requested URL, response status, browser or device information, and security or diagnostic events. This is standard connection data needed to deliver, protect, and troubleshoot an online service.

Offline use

The PRG32 runtime and offline games do not require an account. Settings and saved progress may be stored locally on your device. PRG32 does not receive that local data unless you choose a connected or upload feature.

04

Where legitimate interests apply, we balance those interests against your rights and reasonable expectations. PRG32 does not sell personal data, use it for third-party targeted advertising, or make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects solely by automated means.

05

Cookies and tracking

This public website does not set non-essential cookies and contains no advertising or behavioural analytics. Because consent-requiring tracking is not used here, no cookie-consent banner is shown.

Connected Services may use strictly necessary storage for authentication, security, a shopping basket, or a feature you explicitly request. Any non-essential analytics, personalisation, or similar tracking must remain disabled until the user has made a freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous choice. A connected Service that introduces such technology must disclose it and provide equally accessible accept and reject controls before activation.

06

Recipients and transfers

Public cartridge listings, creator information you choose to publish, and leaderboard entries are visible to other users. Multiplayer data is shared with session participants as needed to provide the feature.

Hosting, email, authentication, storage, security, and technical-support providers may process data only as needed to provide their service and under appropriate data-protection terms. Data may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We aim to process data in the European Economic Area. If a provider transfers personal data outside the EEA, the transfer must rely on a valid GDPR mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses, together with supplementary safeguards where required. You may request information about the relevant safeguards using the contact details below.

07

Retention and security

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected: account data while the account is active and for a limited period needed to handle closure; uploaded public content until it is removed or the account is deleted, subject to backups and legal claims; support and rights-request records while the request is handled and for the period needed to demonstrate compliance; and security logs only for the shortest operational period compatible with incident detection and investigation.

Exact periods may differ when a specific Service states a shorter period or law requires a longer one. Periods are reviewed using purpose, sensitivity, security risk, dispute limitation periods, and legal obligations as criteria. Data is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed.

We apply proportionate technical and organisational measures, including access controls, encrypted transport, data minimisation, maintained software, backups, and incident handling. No system can guarantee absolute security; please use unique credentials and do not upload confidential data to public fields.

08

Children and education

PRG32 is educational and may be used in classrooms. Offline features can be used without an account. Children should use connected Services with the involvement of a parent, guardian, teacher, or school and in line with applicable age-of-consent rules.

We do not knowingly process a child’s personal data in breach of applicable law. A parent, guardian, or school representative who believes data was provided without the required authorisation may contact us for review and deletion.

09

Your GDPR rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction, and portability; object to processing based on legitimate interests; and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. You also have the right not to be subject to a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects.

Send a request to the address below. We may ask for information necessary to verify your identity. We will respond without undue delay and ordinarily within one month; the GDPR permits an extension for complex or numerous requests, in which case we will explain the delay.

You may lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali or with the supervisory authority in the EU/EEA country where you live, work, or believe an infringement occurred.

10

Changes to this notice

We review this notice when Services, processors, retention practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date and version identify the current notice. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate website or Service notice before they take effect where required.
